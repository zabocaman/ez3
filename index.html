How we work

From client work to shipped technology, in one loop.

The whole model rests on discipline in a specific order. Here's what actually happens, step by step.

01

Agency work funds the runway

Client retainers in paid media, social, brand and web development come in first. This is real, invoiced revenue — not a projection.

02

A share is reserved for innovation

A defined slice of agency profit is set aside specifically to fund innovation-lab projects, released only after client obligations are covered.

03

Partners bring a project, we bring structure

An innovation partner brings an existing project or prototype. EasierWorks brings the legal entity, IP protection, seed capital and admin.

04

Ownership stays majority-partner

The partner holds 60–70% of their project's equity and controls how it's built. EasierWorks holds a minority stake for what it provided.

05

What ships feeds back into the brand

A shipped project brings attention, talent and credibility back to the agency — which brings in the next client, and the next project.

Working legally, on purpose

You're an owner, not unpaid staff.

Equity never substitutes for wages on directed work — that's an Ontario employment-standards violation, and it's the exact trap most "join us, get equity" arrangements fall into.

On your own project

Owner-operator

You set your own hours and methods, and are compensated in majority equity that vests over time — because you're building your own asset, not taking direction.

On agency work

Always paid

Anyone doing directed client work is a paid contractor or employee — fee, invoice, or wage. Equity is never offered in place of pay for directed work.
